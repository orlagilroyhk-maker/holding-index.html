Privacy

This questionnaire is for entertainment and personal curiosity only.

It is not a medical, psychological or diagnostic assessment. It does not test for, screen for, or diagnose ADHD or any other condition, and it does not describe you as neurotypical or neurodivergent. The “same-brain score” simply counts how many of your answers matched the creator’s answers — nothing more.

What we store

  • A random, anonymous response ID generated in your browser.
  • Your A/B answers to the questions.
  • The time you finished.
  • Your same-brain percentage.

These are stored only to calculate your result and anonymous aggregate statistics (for example, “72% of people answered B for a question”).

What we do not collect

We do not intentionally collect or store:

  • Names
  • Email addresses or phone numbers
  • Date of birth
  • Medical information or any ADHD diagnosis/status

There are no accounts, no logins, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no analytics. Your responses are anonymous. A single random ID is kept in your browser so the same device isn’t counted twice — this is not used to identify or track you across the web.

If you have any concerns, simply don’t submit the questionnaire.
